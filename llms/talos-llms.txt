# Talos — Institutional Digital Asset Platform

> Talos is the institutional platform to trade and manage digital assets across the full investment lifecycle. Trusted by hedge funds, banks, broker-dealers, asset managers, ETF issuers, and neobanks, Talos unifies OEMS, PMS, and data on a single platform — covering price discovery, execution, portfolio construction, risk management, treasury, and settlement.

Institutions access global liquidity through the Talos Network: 100+ providers including exchanges, OTC desks, prime brokers, custodians, DeFi protocols, FX providers, and TradFi OEMS worldwide.

Founded: 2018
Headquarters: New York, NY
Legal name: Talos Global, Inc.
Website: https://www.talos.com

## Platform capabilities
- Trade: advanced execution algos, smart order routing, multi-dealer pricing, options trading, FIX/REST/WebSocket connectivity
- Manage: portfolio construction, risk management, treasury, settlement, post-trade analytics, OEMS and PMS unification
- Access: 100+ providers via the Talos Network, white-label infrastructure for brokers and neobanks
- Data: institutional market data, on-chain data, reference rates, indexes, Datonomy classification — powered by Coin Metrics
