The CMBI Candidate Market Guidelines define how Talos evaluates and selects the constituent markets used as data inputs for the CMBI Benchmarks. Talos is proposing an update to the exchange eligibility criteria used in the CMBI Candidate Market Guidelines. In accordance with our CMBI Index Policies, Talos is consulting the market regarding this proposed update
