Ten years ago, the perpetual swap was introduced. BitMEX wanted to offer leveraged bitcoin exposure without expiries, delivery or a settlement calendar, so it listed a future that never settles and used a funding payment to hold it near spot. This innovation is now the dominant way crypto trades, and it is starting to appear across real-world assets.
