For years, the prospect of banks and other institutions moving into the digital assets space has been one of the industry's most talked about and closely watched developments. That shift is now becoming increasingly visible as banks, asset managers, ETF issuers, and brokerages move from exploring digital assets to building long-term businesses around them.
